Try it

DMA troll blocks the bridge. "Nobody crosses without paying the toll," it growls. What do you do?

Notice you didn't need a single rule. You said what you wanted, rolled when asked, and the DM did the rest.

The DM does the heavy lifting

All the rules, numbers and tricky stuff live in the Dungeon Master's head. Your only job is to answer one question: "What do you do?"

The DM handles

  • The rules
  • The world
  • Every other character
  • The monsters
  • When to roll dice
  • What the dice mean

You handle

  • Saying what you do.

Sometimes the DM will say "roll this die." You roll it. The DM tells you what it means. That's it.

Nothing to learn. Nothing to bring.
